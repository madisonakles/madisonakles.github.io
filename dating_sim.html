This simulation tests Automation Bias in high-stakes social decision making.

Parasuraman & Manzey (2010) →
2. Algorithm Aversion (The Flipside) Conversely, seeing an algorithm make a mistake (a "bad match") can lead users to rapidly lose trust in the model entirely.

Dietvorst, Simmons, & Massey (2015) →
3. Algorithmic Appreciation Despite stated aversion, people often rely more on algorithmic advice than human advice when the task is objective.

Logg, Minson, & Moore (2019) →
