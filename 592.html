The Ethics Pulse-Check

1. Honestly, how much do you think your data is actually private today?

2. Do you log into personal accounts (banking, social) on work devices?

3. Do you have a piece of tape or a slider over your laptop camera?

4. Do you ever say "Oh, my algorithm is so good today!" regarding TikTok/Spotify?

5. Does it bother you that Alexa/Siri seems to know you're about to speak before you even say the wake word?

6. You talk about a niche product at dinner, and 20 minutes later you see an Instagram ad for it. Your reaction?

7. You're at an airport with 1% battery and need to check your email. Do you join the "Free_Public_WiFi" with no password?
