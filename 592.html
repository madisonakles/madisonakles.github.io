The Ethics Pulse-Check

A quick diagnostic of your digital footprint.

1. On a scale of 1 to 5, how much do you believe your personal data is private today?

2. Do you log into personal accounts on your work computer?

3. Do you have a physical cover (tape/slider) over your webcam?

4. Have you ever said "my algorithm is so good today" or similar?

5. Do you have an Amazon Alexa or Echo Dot in your home?

6. Do you "Accept Cookies" without reading what they are?

7. Do you actually know what "Accept Cookies" means for your data?
