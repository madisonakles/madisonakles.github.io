🧠 Research Gaps & Literature Context

1. Expressive vs. Strategic Sharing:
Current literature (e.g., Van Bavel & Pereira, 2018) suggests users share misinformation to "signal" group loyalty. This simulation isolates "Strategic" sharing—where the user knows the info might be false but shares it to achieve professional utility.

2. The "Accuracy Nudge" Limitation:
While Pennycook & Rand (2021) focus on "Accuracy Nudges" for the general public, this 'War Room' simulation asks: Does the pressure to 'win' in a high-stakes role override the cognitive capacity for verification?

📝 Future Direction: Counter-Attitudinal Role-Playing
A critical next step is assigning users to the opposing political party (e.g., a Democrat playing as a Republican Manager). This tests Cognitive Dissonance: Does "Professional Duty" override personal moral alignment?
