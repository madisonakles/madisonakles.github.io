Network Generalization

This task measures the Generalization Gradient.

In Zhang & Hackel (2026), they propose that in high-density groups, we form a gradient of avoidance.

The Metric: We are measuring if your rejection rate drops linearly as the "network distance" from the bad actor increases (Degree 1 → Degree 2 → Degree 3).

Hypothesis:
Users with high "Cognitive Load" will over-generalize, rejecting everyone in the cluster regardless of distance.
