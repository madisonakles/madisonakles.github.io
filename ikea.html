🧩 The "IKEA Effect" in Algorithms

Does the effort of "building" an algorithm make us more likely to accept its flaws?

The Hypothesis: Participants in Condition A (High Ownership), who spent time "training" the system, will significantly favor the algorithm's output—including the mismatched "Nuclear" suggestion—compared to Condition B, despite both receiving identical results.

This tests if Self-AI Overlap overrides logical preference consistency.

Theoretical Basis:
  • The IKEA Effect: Norton, Mochon, & Ariely (2012) on valuation of self-made products.
  • Psychological Ownership: Morewedge et al. (2021).
  • AI Responsibility: Baek et al. (2026) on AI Customization & Responsibility Attribution.
*This is a functional prototype. No actual data is being collected.
