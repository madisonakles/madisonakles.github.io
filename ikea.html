🧩 The "IKEA Effect" in Algorithms

The Evaluation Step: You just rated the alignment of the portfolio.

The Hypothesis: Participants in Condition A (High Ownership), who spent time "training" the system, consistently rate the portfolio significantly higher than Condition B—even though the results (including the mismatched "Nuclear" suggestion) are identical.
